\begin{cabstract}
  吊钩端近场空间是塔吊吊装过程中碰撞风险最集中的区域，将激光雷达与工业相机直接部署于吊钩端可缩短传感距离，但吊钩端持续三维运动导致传感器观测基准动态变化，异构传感器时空一致性表征与碰撞风险协同判定是亟待解决的关键技术难点。

  围绕上述问题，本文构建了以双激光雷达与工业相机为核心的集成式吊钩端感知系统，从多传感器时空标定、融合点云定位与动态目标检测、雷视融合分级预警三个层面展开研究。在感知层面，提出基于分割掩码的点云投影回查方法，将二维视觉语义转化为三维实例点云集合，构建包络球与包络圆柱相结合的双基准安全包络模型，实现吊装构件的空间定位与几何表达；在预警层面，建立基于三维净空与碰撞时间协同判定的分级预警机制，形成涵盖感知、定位、判级与控制建议的完整预警闭环。

  实际施工场景实验结果表明，点云投影关联精确率达92.17\%，F1分数84.69\%，验证了语义-几何跨模态映射的准确性；动态风险分级准确率达86.65\%，误报率5.35\%，漏报率8.00\%，在相向接近工况下可实现0.70\,s至1.60\,s的提前预警；全链路端到端时延均值101.43\,ms，满足实时性要求。研究结果表明，本文方法能够在施工吊装场景下实现稳定的吊钩端近场感知与可靠的风险分级预警，为塔吊吊装过程的安全监控提供了具有工程可行性的技术方案。
\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
  The near-field space around the hook end is the most collision-concentrated region during tower crane hoisting. Deploying LiDAR and industrial cameras directly at the hook end can physically shorten the sensing distance, but the continuous three-dimensional motion of the hook end causes the sensor observation reference frame to change dynamically. Achieving consistent spatiotemporal representation of heterogeneous sensor data and collaborative collision risk assessment under such highly dynamic conditions is a key technical challenge.

  To address this problem, an integrated hook-end perception system comprising dual LiDARs and an industrial camera was developed, with research conducted at three levels: multi-sensor spatiotemporal calibration, fused point cloud localization and dynamic object detection, and LiDAR-vision fusion-based graded warning. At the perception level, a segmentation mask-based point cloud projection retrieval method was proposed to transform 2D visual semantics into 3D instance point clouds, and a dual-baseline safety envelope model combining a bounding sphere and a bounding cylinder was constructed for spatial localization and geometric representation of hoisting components. At the warning level, a graded warning mechanism jointly determined by 3D clearance and time-to-collision (TTC) was established, forming a complete closed loop of perception, localization, grading, and control suggestion.

  Experimental validation in actual construction scenarios yielded the following results: point cloud projection association achieved a precision of 92.17\% and an F1-score of 84.69\%, confirming the accuracy of semantic-to-geometric cross-modal mapping; dynamic risk grading accuracy reached 86.65\% with a false-alarm rate of 5.35\% and a miss rate of 8.00\%, achieving early warning lead times of 0.70\,s to 1.60\,s in opposing approach scenarios; the end-to-end pipeline latency averaged 101.43\,ms, meeting real-time requirements. These results demonstrate that the proposed method can achieve stable hook-end near-field perception and reliable risk graded warning in construction hoisting scenarios, offering an engineering-feasible technical solution for safety monitoring of tower crane hoisting operations.
\end{eabstract}

\ekeywords{LiDAR-vision fusion, tower crane hoisting, hook-end perception, dynamic obstacle avoidance, risk early warning}
